# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate in project discussions and code
review. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and
publication of another person's private information are not accepted.

Assume good intent while discussing technical decisions, focus criticism on
the work, and give contributors a reasonable opportunity to correct mistakes.
Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer through the repository
owner's verified contact channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical. This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the
project elsewhere.
